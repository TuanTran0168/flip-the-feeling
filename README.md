# Flip the Feeling 🎵

**Flip the Feeling** is an immersive emotional music space where the melodies of **Bui Anh Tuan** help you touch the depths of your soul.

## 🌟 A New Way to Experience Music

The application is designed as a journey of self-discovery through virtual cards. Here, you don't just listen to music; you experience it:

- **Listen to Your Heart:** Choose from 8 emotional states, from *Loneliness* and *Longing* to *Peace* and *Hope*.
- **Artistic Interaction:** Experience the sensation of flipping soft 3D cards to reveal a song specifically chosen for your mood.
- **Immersive Atmosphere:** Lose yourself in a shimmering starfield where sound and visuals blend for ultimate relaxation.

*For those searching for a melody to soothe the soul.*
